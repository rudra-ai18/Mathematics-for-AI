# Day 01 Practice

Solve these without looking at the answers first.

1. Find 15% of 240.
2. Convert 3/8 into a decimal and percentage.
3. Find the average of 8, 12, 15, and 5.
4. A model predicts 17 out of 20 samples correctly. Find its accuracy.
5. Explain why percentages help compare datasets of different sizes.

## Answers

1. 36
2. 0.375 = 37.5%
3. 10
4. 85%
5. Percentages normalize a quantity relative to a total, making comparisons easier.
